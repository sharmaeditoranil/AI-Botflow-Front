Module 07 Route: /settings?tab=whatsapp

WhatsApp Official Cloud API Configuration

To send and receive WhatsApp messages via certified Meta servers, connect your Meta Developer account by following these exact steps:

Step 1: Create a Meta Developer Business App

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com and click "My Apps" > "Create App".
  2. Select "Business" as your app type and assign your App Name.
  3. In the App Dashboard product list, locate WhatsApp and click "Set Up".

Step 2: Retrieve API Credentials

  1. In the left sidebar, navigate to WhatsApp > API Setup.
  2. Phone Number ID: Copy the 15-digit number under Phone Number ID.
  3. WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID: Copy the 15-digit WABA ID.
  4. Permanent Access Token: Do not use temporary 24-hour tokens. In Meta Business Manager, create a System User with permissions:
    whatsapp_business_messaging, whatsapp_business_management

Step 3: Save Credentials in Ai Botflow

Paste the Phone Number ID, WABA ID, and Permanent Token into their respective fields inside Ai Botflow Settings and click "Save WhatsApp Credentials".

Step 4: Configure Meta Webhook for Real-Time Incoming Messages

  1. Copy the Webhook URL provided in your CRM:
    https://dash.aibotflow.in/api/whatsapp/webhook
  2. Copy the unique Webhook Verify Token displayed on screen.
  3. In Meta Developer Portal, go to WhatsApp > Configuration > Edit Webhook.
  4. Paste the Callback URL and Verify Token, then click "Verify and Save".
  5. Under Webhook fields, click "Manage" and check the box for messages.

Step 5: Verify Live Connection

Click "Test Connection" in Ai Botflow. You will receive a green `✓ Connected` badge confirming real-time bidirectional syncing.

Module 09 Route: /settings?tab=templates

Meta WhatsApp Templates & Variable Mapping

Meta enforces strict pre-approved message templates whenever initiating outbound broadcasts or contacting customers outside the active 24-hour service window.

Syncing Existing Templates From Meta

Click "Sync with Meta". Ai Botflow immediately imports all approved templates, languages, categories (Marketing, Utility, Authentication), and interactive button setups from your WABA account, displaying live status chips (APPROVED, REJECTED, or PENDING).

Creating a New Template Inside CRM

  1. Click "+ Create New Template".
  2. Select Category: Marketing (promotions, offers), Utility (order updates, receipts), or Authentication (OTPs).
  3. Enter Template Name using lowercase letters and underscores only (e.g. festive_discount_offer).
  4. Choose Language (e.g. English US, Hindi, etc.).
  5. Header (Optional): Select Text Header or Media Header (Image, PDF Document, or Video).
  6. Body Text with Contiguous Variables: Use the quick insertion buttons:
    {{1}} Customer Name, {{2}} Today's Date, {{3}} Custom Attribute
    Note: Meta policy strictly mandates that variables must be numbered sequentially without skips (e.g. {{1}}, {{2}}, {{3}}).
  7. Interactive Buttons: Add Quick Reply buttons (e.g. "Interested", "Stop") or Call-to-Action buttons (Website link or Phone number).
  8. Click "Submit to Meta for Approval". Approvals typically clear in 2 minutes to 24 hours.

Custom Variable Data Mapping & Fallbacks

Before launching a broadcast, templates containing variables must be linked to CRM contact fields:

  • Find templates marked with the amber `⚠ Needs Mapping` badge and click "Map Variables".
  • For each placeholder (e.g. {{1}}), select the source field (Full Name, First Name, Phone, Email, Company, or Custom Field).
  • Provide a Fallback Value (e.g. "Valued Customer") in case a contact profile is missing that specific field.
  • Inspect the Live WhatsApp Bubble Preview on the right to verify formatting.
  • Save mapping. The badge turns into a green `✓ Data Mapped` chip ready for broadcasting.

2. WhatsApp Inbound Chat & Auto-Reply (RAG Execution)

  1. Customer Pehchan (Identity Resolution): System check karta hai ki customer pehle se contacts list me exist karta hai ya naya hai. Agar naya hai toh phone number se contact record turant create hota hai.
  2. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Vector Search: AI aapke Knowledge Base (PDF catalogs, service guides, pricing tables, FAQs) me cosine similarity vector search run karta hai aur customer ke query se relevant exact facts retrieve karta hai.
  3. Natural Conversational Reply: AI robot-like generic canned response nahi deta, balki ek professional sales executive ki tarah polite Hindi, English ya Hinglish me instant WhatsApp reply bhejta hai.
  4. Safety Loop Guard: auto_reply_max_per_conversation setting ensure karti hai ki AI kisi loop me na phase.

4. AI Lead Qualification & Pipeline Deals (Zero Junk Rule)

Traditional CRMs me har incoming "Hi" ya "Hello" par fake deal ban jati hai jisse sales pipeline garbage ban jati hai. AI-Botflow me Smart Qualification logic active hai:

  • Single Customer = Single Deal Rule (Auto-Merge): Agar ek customer 5 alag-alag messages bhejta hai ya multiple website forms bharta hai, to uske 5 alag deals create nahi honge. AI existing active deal detect karke usi deal ke andar new notes aur updates merge kar deta hai.
  • High-Intent Deals Only: Deal tabhi create hoti hai jab customer rate, pricing, booking, quotation ya custom service details mangta hai (isInterested = true).
  • No Spam Junk: Casual messages jaise "Hi", "Ok", "Good Morning" par koi deal create nahi hoti. Sirf chat history log hoti hai.
  • Pipeline ID-Based Linking: Deal pipeline ki unique ID se associate hoti hai. Isliye agar aap pipeline ka name change bhi kar dein, tab bhi AI hamesha sahi pipeline ke initial stage me deal route karega.

5. AI Long-Term Customer Memory (ai_memory)

Har contact profile me dedicated ai_memory vector store hota hai:

  • Preference Persistence: Agar customer ne 4 din pehle bataya tha ki "Mujhe 30-sheet wedding album chahiye", toh AI is requirement ko memory me persist karta hai.
  • Contextual Continuity: Agli baar jab customer conversation start karta hai, AI use purane context ke sath personalized treat karta hai.

6. Smart Follow-ups & AI Deal Summarizer

  • AI Summarize Chat Button (Inside Deal Card): Pipeline me deal open karne par "AI Summarize Chat" par click karein. AI customer ke 50+ WhatsApp messages ko 2 second me padh kar actionable follow-up note generate kar deta hai.
  • Auto AI Follow-up (Expected Close Date): Jab deal ki Expected Close Date trigger hoti hai, AI customer ko personalized WhatsApp message automatically dispatch karta hai.
  • Custom Deal Instructions: Deal me custom prompt de sakte hain (e.g. "Offer 10% discount if customer asks about price") aur AI isi instruction ke tahat follow-up drafting karega.

Agar customer WhatsApp par likhta hai "Mujhe human agent se baat karni hai", toh AI chat ko designated human agent ko assign karke instant dashboard alert send karta hai.

4. Triple-Layer Wait Resumption Mechanism

Jab koi workflow wait step par aata hai, toh execution suspend hokar database table automation_pending_executions me run_at timestamp ke sath save ho jati hai. Iske resumption ke liye 3-tier protection active hai:

  • Layer 1 — In-Memory Timer (≤ 15 mins): Node.js runtime me scheduleWaitWakeup timer lagta hai jo short waits ko exact millisecond precision par wake-up karta hai.
  • Layer 2 — Background Database Sweeper (Every 30s): Next.js continuous background worker har 30 seconds me due executions inspect karta hai.
  • Layer 3 — Host Operating System Crontab (Every 1 min): VPS / Server crontab entry (* * * * * curl -s http://localhost:3000/api/automations/cron) ensure karti hai ki server restart ya memory purge ke baad bhi koi bhi follow-up message skip na ho.
  • Atomic Lock Protection: Duplicate execution prevent karne ke liye row ko pehle atomic transaction se status = 'running' claim kiya jata hai.

7. Execution Logs & Troubleshooting

  • 24-Hour Policy Compliance: Customer ke last message ke 24 ghante baad plain text message fail ho jata hai. Hamesha send_template action use karein taaki Meta window bypass ho sake.

Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q1: Will our business phone number get blocked or banned?

No. Ai Botflow connects strictly through the Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Unlike unauthorized Chrome extensions or QR code web session scrapers that result in immediate number bans, the official API is fully certified, legally compliant, and endorsed by Meta.

Q2: What is the 24-hour customer service window?

When a customer messages your WhatsApp number, a 24-hour service window opens. During this 24-hour window, you can exchange free-form text, photos, PDFs, and voice notes without paying Meta conversation fees. Once 24 hours expire without a customer reply, Meta policy requires sending an Approved Template Message to re-open the conversation.

Q3: Why does my template display the amber "Needs Mapping" badge?

If your approved Meta template contains dynamic variables (like {{1}} or {{2}}), the CRM needs to know which customer field to insert into those blanks. Click "Map Variables" on the template, select the source field (e.g. Full Name), provide a fallback value (e.g. "Valued Customer"), and save. The badge will turn green ✓ Data Mapped.

Q4: What file formats are supported for Broadcast media headers?

  • Images: JPEG, PNG (Up to 5MB)
  • Documents: PDF, Word DOCX (Up to 16MB)
  • Videos: MP4, 3GP (Up to 16MB)

All media files can be uploaded directly via drag-and-drop inside our Broadcast wizard without needing any external CDN URL.
