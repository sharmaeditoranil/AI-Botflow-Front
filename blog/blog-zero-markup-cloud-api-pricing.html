1. The Hidden BSP Markup Model: How Intermediaries Profit

Traditionally, when you registered for WhatsApp Business API, you had to go through a legacy on-premise Business Solution Provider (BSP). Because the BSP managed the hosting server, they acted as a billing middleman:

  • Meta charges ₹0.78 for an Indian marketing conversation.
  • The intermediary BSP bills your credit card ₹0.99 to ₹1.15 per message.
  • They pocket the ₹0.21 to ₹0.37 difference as a "convenience margin."
  • On top of this, you are forced to prepay credits in advance into a closed wallet system with high minimum reloads.
