Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, and WhatsApp messaging rates.

Are Service Chat messages free?
The first 1,000 Service Chat messages each month are included free. After the monthly allowance, standard Service Chat rates apply (₹0.35/message).
What does Meta charge on top of the plan?
WhatsApp conversation charges are billed by Meta at their standard published rates. You top up a prepaid wallet directly inside your dashboard and see the exact cost per campaign before sending. AiBotFlow charges ZERO per-message platform markup.
Can I change plans later?
Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes apply seamlessly from the next billing cycle and your configured flows, contacts, AI agent memory, and chat history stay completely intact.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fee. Self-service onboarding, official Meta Cloud API number verification, template approval support, and flow builder access are included on every tier, and on your 14-day free trial.
Will our WhatsApp number get blocked?
AiBotFlow connects strictly through the Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Operating on the official API helps businesses operate in full accordance with Meta's messaging policies, avoiding the permanent phone number bans caused by unofficial scrapers and browser extensions.
What happens after my 14-day free trial ends?
You get full access during the 14-day free trial without needing credit card details. When your trial ends, you can choose Starter (₹799/mo), Growth (₹1,499/mo), or Enterprise (₹2,999/mo) to keep your automations running smoothly.
