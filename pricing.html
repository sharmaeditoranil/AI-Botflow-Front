Clear Answers

Pricing Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about our subscription tiers, Meta charges, wallet top-ups, and cancellations.

What does Meta charge on top of the plan?
WhatsApp conversation charges are billed directly by Meta at their published official rates. In AI Botflow, you simply top up your prepaid messaging wallet in the dashboard and see the exact cost per campaign before sending. AI Botflow charges ZERO per-message platform markup.
Can I change plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time with one click from your Billing Settings tab. Any upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing, while your active flows, contacts, AI agent training vectors, and chat history remain completely intact.
Is there any setup or onboarding fee?
No, there are zero setup fees. Guided onboarding, Meta Business API number verification, and template approval support are fully included on every plan, as well as during your 14-day free trial.
Do you offer annual invoicing and GST tax invoices for businesses?
Yes! Yearly billing saves you ~17% (2 months free on every plan). All plans come with automatic GST-compliant tax invoices that you can download directly from your dashboard for business accounting. Custom enterprise purchase orders and annual bank transfer invoicing are also available.
Will our WhatsApp number get blocked?
No. AI Botflow connects strictly through the Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Unlike unauthorized Chrome extensions or QR code web-scrapers that trigger permanent phone number bans, the official Cloud API is fully authorized, backed by Meta's compliance safeguards, and approved for high-throughput business communication.
What happens after my 14-day free trial ends?
You get full access to all features during the 14-day free trial without needing to enter credit card details. When your trial ends, you can choose any plan (Starter ₹599, Growth ₹1,499, or Enterprise ₹2,499) to keep your automations running smoothly. If you don't upgrade, your account is safely paused without data loss.
