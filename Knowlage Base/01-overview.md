# Platform Overview & Architecture
**Route:** `/` (Dashboard)

**AI Botflow WhatsApp CRM** is an enterprise-grade customer communication and automation platform built directly on Meta's Official Cloud API.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Zero Number Ban Risk**: 100% official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API.
- **Unified Team Inbox**: Shared inbox for multiple agents with customer tagging, quick replies, and internal notes.
- **Bulk WhatsApp Broadcasts**: Send 10,000+ personalized messages with direct Image/Video/PDF file uploads without external URLs.
- **Smart Variable Mapping**: Map CRM fields (Name, Date, Custom Fields) with automatic fallback values for Meta templates.
- **Visual Drag & Drop Flow Builder**: Build interactive 24/7 button & list menu chatbots without writing code.
- **Generative AI Agents**: Train AI on your custom business documents, FAQs, and product catalogs for intelligent human-like auto-replies.
- **Webhook Integrations**: Connect Shopify, WooCommerce, WordPress, Zapier, and custom webhooks for instant order & lead alerts.
