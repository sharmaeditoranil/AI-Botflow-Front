# Registration & Signup
**Route:** `/signup`

1. Visit **[dash.aibotflow.in/signup](https://dash.aibotflow.in/signup)**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Business Email Address**, and **Password**.
3. **Password Strength**: The meter will indicate password security (Minimum 8 characters, numbers, and symbols recommended).
4. Click **"Create Account"**.
5. **On-Screen OTP Verification**: A 6-digit verification code is instantly sent to your email inbox (via high-speed custom Gmail SMTP).
6. Enter the 6-digit OTP and click **"Verify & Continue"**. Your account is immediately activated.
