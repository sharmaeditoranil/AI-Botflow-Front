# Instagram & Facebook Messenger Channels
**Route:** `/settings?tab=social`

Connect Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional accounts to handle Instagram DMs and Messenger chats in the same unified inbox.

1. Click **"Connect Facebook / Instagram"**.
2. Log in with your Facebook account that manages your business pages.
3. Select the Facebook Page and linked Instagram Business Account.
4. Grant messaging permissions.
5. All incoming DMs will now arrive directly in your **Live Inbox (`/inbox`)** tagged with an `Instagram` or `Messenger` badge.
