# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Troubleshooting
**Route:** `/contact` (Help Center)

#### Q1: Will our WhatsApp number get blocked?
**No.** AI Botflow connects strictly through the **Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API**. Unlike unauthorized Chrome extensions or QR code scanners that lead to number bans, the official API is fully authorized and protected by Meta.

#### Q2: What is the 24-hour customer service window?
When a customer sends you a message, a 24-hour service window opens. During this window, you can send any free-form messages, images, documents, or voice notes. Once 24 hours pass without a customer reply, Meta requires an **Approved Template Message** to re-engage the customer.

#### Q3: Why is my broadcast showing "Needs Mapping"?
If your chosen template contains dynamic variables like `{{1}}` or `{{2}}`, the CRM needs to know which customer field to insert into those blanks. Click **"Map Variables"** on the template, select the corresponding contact field (e.g., Full Name) and fallback text, then save.

#### Q4: What file formats are supported for Broadcast media headers?
- **Images**: JPEG, PNG (Up to 5MB)
- **Documents**: PDF, Word DOCX (Up to 16MB)
- **Videos**: MP4, 3GP (Up to 16MB)
All files can be uploaded directly via drag-and-drop inside the Broadcast wizard without needing any external CDN or public URL.
