# Billing, Plans & Payment Upgrades
**Route:** `/settings?tab=billing`, `/pricing`

Manage your subscription tier, billing cycles, and payment methods.

- **Available Plans**:
  - **Starter**: Ideal for small businesses (1 WhatsApp Number, 1,000 Contacts, 2 Team Members).
  - **Growth / Pro**: Growing brands (Unlimited Contacts, 5 Team Members, AI Agents, Broadcasts).
  - **Enterprise**: High-volume enterprises (Custom API limits, Dedicated support, Unlimited Agents).
- **Payment Method**: Secure checkout via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking).
- **Invoices**: Download GST-compliant tax invoices directly from the Billing tab.
