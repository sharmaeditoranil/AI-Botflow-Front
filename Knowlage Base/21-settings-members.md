# Team Members, Roles & Permissions
**Route:** `/settings?tab=members`

Collaborate with your sales and support team with granular access control.

### Available Roles:
- **Owner**: Full platform access, billing management, project ownership transfer.
- **Admin**: Can manage templates, broadcasts, automations, team settings, and all chats.
- **Manager**: Can view all conversations, assign agents, and manage contacts.
- **Agent**: Can only access the Inbox, reply to assigned chats, and view contacts.

### Inviting Team Members:
1. Click **"Invite Member"**.
2. Enter the teammate's email address and assign their role.
3. The teammate receives an invitation link (`/join/[token]`) to set up their login and join your workspace.
