# Omnichannel Team Inbox & Live Chat
**Route:** `/inbox`

A collaborative multi-agent inbox designed for fast customer support and sales conversion.

### Inbox Navigation & Filters
- **Tabs**:
  - **All**: All active customer conversations.
  - **Mine**: Chats assigned to you.
  - **Unassigned**: New inbound conversations needing an agent.
  - **Unread**: Conversations with unread incoming messages.
- **Search**: Search conversations by customer name, phone number, or message text.

### Customer Tag Management Bar (Direct in Chat)
- Directly above the message history, view active tags assigned to the open contact.
- Click **"+ Tag"** to instantly add an existing tag or type a new one and press Enter.
- Click the `✕` on any tag chip to remove it immediately without leaving the chat window.

### Composing & Sending Messages
- **24-Hour Service Window Indicator**:
  - If the customer messaged you within the last 24 hours, you can send free-form text, attachments, audio recordings, and media.
  - If the 24-hour window has expired, an alert notifies you to send an **Approved Template Message** to re-open the conversation.
- **Quick Replies (`/`)**:
  - Type `/` in the message box to open your canned responses list.
  - Select any saved template or shortcut (e.g., `/pricing`, `/bank-details`, `/meeting-link`).
- **Direct Media Attachments**: Click the paperclip icon to upload and send Images (JPEG/PNG), Documents (PDF/Docs), and Audio voice notes.
- **Internal Notes**: Toggle to "Internal Note" mode to leave private comments for team members that the customer cannot see.
