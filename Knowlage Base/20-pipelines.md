# Sales Pipelines & Kanban Deals
**Route:** `/pipelines`

Track customer leads through customizable visual sales stages.

- **Kanban Columns**: Customize pipeline stages (e.g., `New Lead`, `Discovery Call`, `Proposal Sent`, `Negotiation`, `Won`, `Lost`).
- **Create Deal**: Click **"+ New Deal"**, specify Deal Name, Monetary Value, Currency, and associate the Contact.
- **Drag & Drop Progression**: Move deal cards between columns as sales progress.
- **Conversion Analytics**: Track total pipeline value and stage conversion velocity.
