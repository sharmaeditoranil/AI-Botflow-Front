Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about connecting your Google Business Profile, API safety, and review automation.

Q1: Is connecting my Google Business Profile safe and compliant with Google's policies?
Yes, 100% safe. AI Botflow connects strictly through the Official Google Business Profile API (OAuth 2.0). Unlike unauthorized scrapers or third-party bots that violate terms of service, our official API connection has zero risk of profile suspension or penalty.
Q2: Can I customize the AI's tone and keywords for review replies?
Yes! You have full control over the AI's tone (Professional, Warm, Friendly, or Luxury), sign-off signatures, and localized target keywords (e.g. your city name, service names, and doctor/manager names) to ensure every response feels authentic and boosts your local SEO rank.
Q3: What happens when a customer leaves a negative (1-star or 2-star) review?
When an at-risk review is detected, AI Botflow immediately sends an alert to your WhatsApp Team Inbox with customer details and sentiment breakdown. You can choose whether to let the AI post an empathetic, de-escalating reply or hold it for manual manager approval before publishing.
Q4: Can I manage multiple Google My Business locations in one dashboard?
Yes. AI Botflow supports multi-location businesses, retail chains, and agency accounts. You can manage 2, 10, or 200+ location listings, compare rank heatmaps across branches, and manage all customer reviews from one centralized screen.
Q5: How does GMB automation integrate with the WhatsApp CRM?
You can add your verified WhatsApp booking/inquiry link directly to your Google Business Profile call-to-action buttons. When customers click to message you, AI Botflow's autonomous WhatsApp AI agents take over instantly to qualify the lead, provide prices, and confirm appointments 24/7.
