Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about AiBotFlow's WhatsApp CRM and AI automation.

Are Service Chat messages free?
The first 1,000 Service Chat messages each month are included free. After the monthly allowance, standard Service Chat rates apply (₹0.35/message).
How does AiBotFlow connect to WhatsApp?
AiBotFlow connects directly through the Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Operating on the official API helps businesses operate in accordance with Meta's messaging policies, avoiding the permanent phone number bans caused by unofficial scrapers and browser extensions.
Do you charge any per-message markup on WhatsApp?
Zero markup. WhatsApp conversation rates are passed through directly without any platform premium. You top up your balance inside your dashboard and see the exact Meta cost before sending.
Can multiple team members use the same WhatsApp number?
Yes! Our Shared Team Inbox allows multiple agents to log in simultaneously, manage customer conversations, leave internal notes, and assign chats to specific team members.
How does the AI Agent learn our products?
You can upload PDF catalogs, price lists, service documents, or website URLs. AiBotFlow indexes your knowledge into vector embeddings so the AI agent retrieves accurate answers and scores customer buying intent.
