Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the official Meta Cloud API, broadcasts, and compliance.

Q1: Will our WhatsApp number get blocked?
No. AI Botflow connects strictly through the Official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for enterprise-grade WhatsApp Automation. Unlike unauthorized Chrome extensions or QR code web-scrapers that trigger permanent phone number bans, our official API integration is fully authorized and protected by Meta.
Q2: What is the 24-hour customer service window?
When a customer sends you a message, an active 24-hour customer service window opens. During this window, you can send any free-form messages, images, documents, or voice notes. Once 24 hours pass without a customer reply, Meta requires an Approved Template Message to safely re-open the conversation. AI Botflow tracks this countdown timer automatically in your Team Inbox.
Q3: Why does a broadcast show "Needs Mapping"?
If your chosen WhatsApp template contains dynamic variables like {{1}} or {{2}}, the CRM needs to know which customer field to insert into those blanks. Click "Map Variables" on the template, select the corresponding contact field (e.g., Full Name) and fallback text, then save. Once mapped, the status switches to "Ready".
Q4: What file formats are supported for Broadcast media headers?
AI Botflow supports direct drag-and-drop file uploads inside the Broadcast wizard without needing any external CDN or public URL:
  • Images: JPEG, PNG (Up to 5MB)
  • Documents: PDF, Word DOCX (Up to 16MB)
  • Videos: MP4, 3GP (Up to 16MB)
